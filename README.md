# Cyclistic_Ride_Share_Analysis
A project focused on identifying differences between members and casual users in order to convert casuals into members.

## Background
Cyclistic is a bike-share company that operates a fleet of several thousand geotracked bicycles across a network of stations spread throughout Chicago. The director of marketing believes the company’s future success depends on maximizing the number of annual memberships. Therefore, we need to understand how casual riders and members use Cyclistic bikes to design a new marketing strategy for converting casual riders into annual members.

## Business Task
Accelerate revenue and annual membership growth by analyzing aggregate trip metrics to discover distinct usage patterns for casual riders and annual members, equipping marketing teams with data-supported profiles needed to optimize conversion campaigns.

### Key Objectives
1) **Develop Behavioral Profiles**: Segment ride data to isolate casual use that mirrors member behavior.
2) **Optimize Campaign Timing**: Pinpoint high-yield seasonal windows for aggressive marketing by mapping fluctuations in ride volume.
3) **Locate Geographic Hotspots**: Identify high-density stations for high-ROI out-of-home (OOH) advertisements.
4) **Define Conversion Triggers**: Translate clear cost-benefit metrics to establish where annual membership becomes more economical.

## Data and Methodology

### Data Source
The [raw data](https://divvy-tripdata.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html), stripped of personal identifying information, is publicly available where Divvy, the real-world counterpart to the fictional company Cyclistic, publishes their records from June 1, 2020 to present. It is available through Motivate International Inc. under this [license](https://divvybikes.com/data-license-agreement).
